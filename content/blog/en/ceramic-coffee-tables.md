## What is a ceramic coffee table?

A ceramic coffee table has a top made from a ceramic slab instead of wood, glass, or marble. That gives you a table that is easy to maintain and visually luxurious at the same time.

Tiles ceramic coffee tables are handmade in Serbia, with a focus on combining modern design and everyday practicality.

## What are the advantages of a ceramic coffee table?

Ceramic as a coffee-table surface has several concrete benefits:

- **Scratch resistance** – a ceramic surface is harder than wood and far more resistant to everyday wear.
- **Heat resistance** – a hot cup or dish will not leave a mark on the surface.
- **Easy maintenance** – wiping with a damp cloth is enough; no special products are required.
- **Stain resistance** – ceramic does not absorb liquids the way wood or some composites can.
- **Aesthetics** – ceramic slabs can imitate marble, concrete, or wood, with far less maintenance than the original materials.

## Ceramic or marble: which is better for a coffee table?

Marble has traditionally been a synonym for luxury in interiors, but it also has practical limits: it is porous, sensitive to acids (for example lemon juice or wine), and needs regular sealing.

Ceramic can achieve a similar marble look visually, while being more resistant to stains, scratches, and heat. That is why it is increasingly chosen as a marble alternative in modern living rooms, especially in homes with children or frequent table use.

## How do you maintain a ceramic coffee table?

Care is simple:

1. Wipe the surface with a damp cloth.
2. For tougher stains, use a mild cleaner without abrasive ingredients.
3. No wax, oil, or impregnation is needed, unlike wood or marble.

## Which interiors suit a ceramic coffee table best?

A ceramic coffee table is most often chosen for:

- modern and minimalist living rooms,
- interiors of different styles and color palettes, since ceramic surfaces come in a wide range of tones and textures,
- spaces where the table is used every day and practicality matters as much as appearance.

With a beige corner sofa, for example, ceramic surfaces in warm stone or sand tones work especially well — they soften the room visually without adding harsh contrast.
